# MECEE QUICK — 2027 Medical Entrance Preparation Platform

MECEE QUICK is a medical entrance exam preparation platform built for candidates preparing for the **MECEE-BL 2027 (MBBS, BDS, BSc Nursing, B.Pharm, BAMS)** in Nepal.

---

## 🚀 One-Click Deploy to Vercel

This repository is pre-configured for instant **Vercel** deployment:

1. Push this repository to **GitHub**.
2. Go to [Vercel Dashboard](https://vercel.com/new).
3. Import this repository.
4. Vercel will automatically detect **Vite**:
   - **Framework Preset**: `Vite`
   - **Build Command**: `npm run build`
   - **Output Directory**: `dist`
5. Click **Deploy**!

Your live site will be ready at `https://your-project.vercel.app` in under 1 minute.

## 🎯 Key Features Included

- **Full 200-Mark Grand Mock**: Official 200 questions, 3 hours (180 mins) countdown, +1.0 for correct, -0.25 negative marking penalty.
- **Official 2027 Marks Distribution**:
  - **Zoology (40 Marks)**: Human Biology (15M), Selected Animals (6M), Animal Diversity (4M), Tissues (4M), Diseases (4M), Evolution (3M), MedTech (2M), Biota (2M).
  - **Botany (40 Marks)**: Biodiversity (9M), Genetics (6M), Plant Physiology (6M), Cell Bio (5M), Ecology (4M), Anatomy (3M), Applied (3M), Components of Life (2M), Developmental (2M).
  - **Chemistry (50 Marks)**: Physical Chem (17M), Organic Chem (17M), Inorganic (10M), Applied (3M), Analytical (3M).
  - **Physics (50 Marks)**: Modern Physics (12M), Mechanics (10M), Current Electricity (9M), Wave & Optics (8M), Heat & Thermodynamics (7M), Electrostatics (4M).
  - **MAT (20 Marks)**: Verbal (5M), Numerical (5M), Logical (5M), Spatial (5M).
- **The Big Priority List**: Strategic focus on the Top 12 units accounting for over 70% of the entire exam.
- **Categorized Subject & Unit Quizzes**: Instant feedback mode (with concepts revealed) and timed exam modes.
- **Self-Review & Penalty Diagnostics**: In-depth review with explanations, filtering by Incorrect (-0.25 penalty), Skipped, and Bookmarked.
- **Local Storage Persistence**: Test attempts, performance records, and question bookmarks stored offline in your browser.
- **Bulk PDF / Text Extractor**: Paste raw extracted questions from syllabus PDFs or notes to expand your question bank with 1 click.
